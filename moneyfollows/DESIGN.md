# MoneyFollows Design System

MoneyFollows follows Impeccable's Neo Kinpaku direction, adapted for a personal finance app.

## Tokens

- Base: lacquer black in dark mode and warm champagne paper in light mode.
- Primary action: kinpaku gold.
- Support accent: verdigris patina for positive states and money growth.
- Expense accent: clear coral red, used only for spending, delete, and error states.
- Avoid generic blue and default gray as the dominant UI color.

## Typography

- Body: Albert Sans.
- Display: Alumni Sans for brand and major totals.
- Keep interface copy compact. Data and controls should scan quickly on mobile.

## Components

- Panels use restrained radii between 8px and 10px.
- Primary buttons use gold fill and dark text.
- Inputs use dark lacquer or light paper surfaces with visible focus.
- Cards are reserved for summaries, entries, notes, reminders, account surfaces, and meme media.
- Motion should be short, calm, and purposeful.

## Layout

- Desktop uses the full browser surface with a side rail and wide content area.
- Mobile switches to a bottom navigation rail and compact stacked sections.
- Every screen should remain usable at 320px width with no horizontal scrolling.
